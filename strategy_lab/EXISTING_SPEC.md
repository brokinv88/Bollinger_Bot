Purpose: audit actual source definitions BASE/B, Donchian55/Keltner H4, correct execution/accounting, then evaluate additions. Do not modify existing config, databases, paper processes or enable live orders. New results use the verified 12-symbol cohort and raw sources of strategy_lab/data (2021–2026-08); they are not a reproduction of the original present-top100 historical universe. Source spot backtest excludes hard stops while paper includes them; test the paper-intended version explicitly and provide a no-hard-stop diagnostic.

Data: spot signal D1 resampled from spot H4, barriers executed on H4. Futures signal H4 resampled from actual USD-M H1, barriers executed on H1, actual funding events and mark series. Only complete UTC signal bars; decision at signal close -> next execution OPEN. ATR legacy = SMA14 true range (NOT Wilder ATR); ADX legacy = EWM alpha1/14 seeded first valid; Bollinger stdev sample ddof1. 200 full D1 bars required spot, 200 H4 futures for stable EMA/ADX warmup. Simultaneous spot candidates ROC20 descending then symbol (paper priority); futures symbol alphabetical, DON before KELT if simultaneous shared-symbol signal. All choices before new results.

BASE: upper=SMA20(high)+1.5*sampleSD20(high); lower=SMA20(low)-1.5*sampleSD20(low). Price above all SMA50/100/150/200 and close crosses upper. Same full filter on pyramids, max3 lots and10 coins. Exit close cross below any SMA or lower -> next D1 open. Hard stop=.99*max(lower, SMAs below signal close), reset at next D1 open and may decrease (matching auto_trade.py). Reference native cash50 per layer (including entry fee), total deployed cost cap1500, starting sleeve3000. No assumed midpoint fill; market next open+cost.
B: BASE plus ROC5<20%, ROC20<40%, SMA_ATR14/close<6%; applies to EVERY layer. Original research_improvements applies these only first entry, does not enforce cash ledger, includes no hardstop by default; differences are explicitly disclosed.
B_SAFE: B entries, same cash50 per layer, stop may only tighten; add layers only when prior completed D1 close > average entry + (sum original stop-risk-distance*qty)/totalqty (at least1R price profit). Full B filters still required. No BTC filter, so effect of safer stops/pyramids can be isolated.
B_REGIME: B_SAFE + prior completed BTC D1 close>SMA200 (same completed day signal). Native risk sizing $20 at initial spot sleeve3000, scales with sleeve equity; max25% sleeve notional per coin, cap1500 deployed cost, max2% sleeve planned remaining-stop-loss exposure; pyramid new risk must fit same symbol25% and total2%. Risk reference excludes unbounded future gap/funding; costs included. Evaluate B_REGIME also at FIXED cash50 (equal sizing diagnostic) to separate capital deployment from signal edge. No further threshold search.

DON55: long H4 close>prior55high and close>EMA50; short mirror. ATR%>SMA50(ATR%), ADX>=20. Last actual funding rate known at signal H4 OPEN <=+0.0005 for long / >=-0.0005 for short (one-sided as source). Initial stop2*entryATR; fixed target7*entryATR (=3.5R), trail4*entryATR. Break-even after signed CLOSE profit>=2*entryATR (=1R gross), activated only next bar, price entry (not fee-free). Preserve trigger levels; fix abs(adverse move), same-bar trailing, gaps, funding, accounting. Signal is level condition, not forced fresh crossover (source behavior).

Other stop/target/trailing unchanged. Both variants declared before results; not renamed winners after the fact.
